Tiene Nombre Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Tiene Nombre Publisher ("the App") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Tiene Nombre channel to publish their own videos.

1. Data we access. When the owner connects their TikTok account, the App receives basic account information (display name, avatar, open ID) and an access token, only to publish videos to that same account.

2. How we use it. The data is used solely to upload and publish videos to the owner's own TikTok account and to check the status of those uploads.

3. Storage. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never shared, sold or sent to third parties.

4. Third parties. The App does not collect data from other TikTok users, does not use analytics or advertising, and does not sell any data.

5. Deletion. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Local tokens are deleted on request.
