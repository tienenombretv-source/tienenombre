Tiene Nombre Publisher: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Tiene Nombre Publisher ("the App") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the "Tiene Nombre" channel to publish their own original videos to their own accounts on TikTok and YouTube. It has no other users.

1. Data we access. When the owner connects an account, the App receives an access token and basic account information: for TikTok, display name, avatar and open ID; for YouTube (through YouTube API Services), the channel ID, title and statistics of the owner's own channel.

2. How we use it. The data is used solely to confirm which account is connected and to upload and publish the owner's own videos (title, description, tags, privacy setting) to that same account, and to check the status of those uploads.

3. Storage. Access and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer only. They are never shared, sold or sent to any third party. No other personal data is stored.

4. Third parties and sharing. The App does not collect data from any other users, does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and does not sell or share any data.

5. YouTube API Services. The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App, users agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data obtained through YouTube API Services is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Revoking access and deletion. The owner can revoke the App's access at any time: for YouTube/Google, from the Google security settings page; for TikTok, from TikTok settings (Security and permissions → Manage app permissions). Locally stored tokens are deleted immediately when access is revoked or on request, and in any case within 7 days.

7. Changes. This policy may be updated; the date above shows the latest version.
